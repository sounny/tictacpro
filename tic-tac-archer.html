Rules

  • Grid is 5x5.
  • Instead of clicking cells, you click arrows around the perimeter.
  • An arrow "shoots" your piece into that row or column.
  • Your piece lands on the first empty cell it hits.
  • If a row or column is full, that arrow is disabled.
  • First to get 4 in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins!
