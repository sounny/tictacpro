How to Play

  • The game is played on a 5x5 grid.
  • Instead of placing pieces, you launch them from the edges.
  • Click the arrow buttons around the board to launch a piece in that direction.
  • Pieces slide until they hit another piece or the edge of the board.
  • First player to get 4 in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins!
