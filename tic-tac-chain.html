How to Play

  • • The game is played on a 4x4 grid.
  • • Players take turns placing their mark (X or O).
  • • Pieces are connected if they are adjacent horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • • Win by creating a connected chain of 5 or more of your pieces.
  • • The chain can be in any shape, not just a straight line!
