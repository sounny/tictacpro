Biological Warfare Rules

  • Grid: 5x5 arena.
  • Winning: Get 4 in a row (placed or infected).
  • Infection: Each placed piece infects 1 random adjacent empty cell every turn.
  • Curing: Place a piece next to an opponent's infected cell to cure it (removes it from the board).
  • Strategy: Infected cells count towards your win, but they are fragile!
