How to Play

  • 🎯 Aim: Move your mouse to aim the crosshair.
  • 🌪️ Wobble: The crosshair drifts randomly. Time your click!
  • 🖱️ Throw: Click to place your piece.
  • 🚫 Miss: Land outside the board to lose your turn.
  • 📈 Difficulty: Wobble increases as the game progresses.
