Rules of Tic Tac Paint

  • • Game is played on a 5x5 grid.
  • • Each turn, claim an empty or faded cell. It becomes a solid cell (1 point).
  • • Placing a solid cell also paints all orthogonally adjacent empty cells with your faded color (0.5 points).
  • • Solid cells are permanent. Faded cells can be overwritten.
  • • Game ends when the board is full (no empty cells). Most points win!
