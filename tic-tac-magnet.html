Magnetic Rules

  • • 4x4 Grid: Get 4 of your pieces in a row to win.
  • • Polarity: Pieces alternate between + and - every turn.
  • • Repel: Placing a piece pushes adjacent pieces of the same polarity away by one cell.
  • • Attract: Placing a piece pulls non-adjacent pieces of the opposite polarity one cell closer.
