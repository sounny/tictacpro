Rules: Tic Tac Shrink

  • Start on a 5x5 grid.
  • After every 4 moves, the outermost ring is removed!
  • Grid shrinks to 3x3, then finally 1x1.
  • Any pieces on removed cells are destroyed.
  • Win: Get 3 in a row on the current board, or be the last player with pieces remaining.
