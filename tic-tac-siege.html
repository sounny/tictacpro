How to Play

  • • Players alternate turns on a 5x5 grid.
  • • On your turn, you can place your piece (X/O) OR a Wall.
  • • Each player has 3 Walls per game.
  • • Walls block a cell permanently and cannot be moved.
  • • First to get 4 in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins!
