Rules of Tic Tac Tsunami

  • Played on a 4x4 grid.
  • First to get 3 in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins.
  • Every 3 total moves, a tsunami wave hits!
  • The tsunami pushes all pieces one cell in a random direction (North, South, East, or West).
  • Pieces pushed off the edge of the board are destroyed.
  • Watch the warning arrow to see which way the next wave will go!
