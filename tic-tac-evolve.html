Rules of Evolution

  • 🌱 Pieces evolve at the start of your turn: Seed (dot) → Sprout (circle) → Bloom (star).
  • ⚔️ Stomping: You can overwrite an opponent's Sprout with your own Seed.
  • 🛡️ Immunity: Seeds cannot be stomped. Blooms are permanent.
  • 🏆 Win by getting 4 Blooms in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally).
