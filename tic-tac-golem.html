Rules of Tic Tac Golem

  • Play on a 4x4 grid.
  • First to get 4 in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins.
  • Every 3 turns, a neutral Golem 🗿 spawns on a random empty cell.
  • Golems block all players and do not count towards winning lines.
  • On your turn, you can either place your mark OR sacrifice your turn to destroy a Golem adjacent to one of your pieces.
