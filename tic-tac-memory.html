Game Rules

  • All cells start face-down.
  • Phase 1: Flip any cell to reveal its content.
  • If the cell is empty, you enter Phase 2: Place your piece there. It stays face-up for 2 turns.
  • If it's an opponent's piece, it flips back and your turn ends immediately.
  • If it's your own piece, it flips back and you can choose a different cell to flip.
  • You must remember where pieces are to build 3 in a row!
