Rules of Tic Tac Tower

  • Classic 3x3 grid, but with a twist!
  • Each cell can hold up to 3 pieces.
  • You can place your piece on top of any stack that isn't full.
  • Only the top piece of each cell counts for winning.
  • Get 3 of your pieces in a row (top level) to win.
  • Strategic stacking lets you reclaim cells from your opponent!
