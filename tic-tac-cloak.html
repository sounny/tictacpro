How to Play Tic Tac Cloak

  • ● Standard Tic Tac Toe rules: 3 in a row wins.
  • ● Each player has 2 Cloak Tokens.
  • ● Use a cloak to make your move invisible to your opponent.
  • ● You can see your own cloaked pieces (they shimmer subtly).
  • ● If an opponent tries to play on a cell containing a hidden piece, the piece is revealed and their turn is wasted!
  • ● Use deduction to figure out where your opponent might be hiding!
