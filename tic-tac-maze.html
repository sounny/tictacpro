Tic Tac Maze Rules

  • • The game is played on a 5x5 grid.
  • • Random walls are generated at the start of each game.
  • • To win, you must get 3 marks in a row (horizontal or vertical).
  • • IMPORTANT: There must be NO WALLS between any of the 3 marks in your winning line.
  • • Diagonals are not allowed in this variant due to the maze walls.
