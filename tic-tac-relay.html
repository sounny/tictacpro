Tournament Rules

Format: Best-of-3 boards. Win 2 boards to win the match.

POWER-UPS:

  • First Strike: The winner of Board 1 gets to place 2 pieces on their first turn of Board 2.

BOARD 3 HAZARD:

Every 3 turns, a random piece on Board 3 teleports to an empty square, unless it is Fortified!
