Rules

  • Played on a 4x4 grid.
  • When you place a piece, a shadow piece of yours also appears on the diagonally opposite cell (mirrored through the center).
  • Shadow pieces are semi-transparent and count toward winning lines.
  • Important: Shadow pieces can be overwritten by a real piece placement (from either player). Real pieces cannot be overwritten.
  • First to get 4 in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins.
